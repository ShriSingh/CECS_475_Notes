#CECS475 #Week8 
By default, a cell's content are aligned horizontally to the left and vertically in the middle
- Use `VALIGN=direction`
	- Direction to change the vertical alignment, where "direction" is top, middle, bottom, or baseline
- Use `ALIGN=direction`
	- Direction to change the horizontal alignment where "direction" is left, center, or right
